Passing it back

Each dot is a household, ageing left to right from 20 to 90; its size is its wealth. Rows are wealth deciles, richest at the top. Older households with something to spare pass ✦ sparkles of it back to younger ones in their row. Tighten the squeeze and watch who can still afford to.

An older household keeps $25k before it gives anything.
Illustrative model, calibrated to ACOSS & UNSW, Inequality in Australia 2024 (wealth by decile and age, ABS 2022-23) and the Productivity Commission, Wealth transfers and their economic effects (2021). One second is one year.
